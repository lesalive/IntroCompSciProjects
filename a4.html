Interactive Art Garden

Instructions:

Bees and rain make flowers grow!

Click and move the cursor around for the bee to follow.

Press 'c' to spawn a cloud.

Use arrow keys to create a gust of wind.

Move your cursor near the ground to change the color of the flowers.

Click on the stats to change the color of the bees or flowers.

Space bar to pause the game. 'X' to clear the paint and score.
